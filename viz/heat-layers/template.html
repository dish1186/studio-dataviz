Heat · what pushes people to act?

Heat layers

Each column is a temperature line from 10 to 115 °F. In every 5° row, circles grow with how often that heat occurred and with how far above normal people searched or sought care in those weeks. The dark dot is the selected week.

Circles built from

Weekly highs: gridMET daily maximum temperature, 7-day mean. Felt heat: UTCI daily maximum, 7-day mean (ends 2026-06-12). Searches: Google Trends "ice cream" and "air conditioner" by metro (Eugene and Bakersfield AC are monthly values). ER: heat-related illness ED visits per 100,000 ED visits, CDC Tracking Network, weekly, by HHS region (Boston Region 1, Detroit Region 5, Eugene Region 10, Bakersfield Region 9). Circle size for searches and ER = how far above the city's typical (median) week their average within-city percentile is in the weeks whose high fell in that row (no circle = typical or below; a solid dot = average in the top fifth); for temperature and felt heat = how many weeks fell in that row. Weeks of 2021-09-26 to 2026-09-20.
