Detroit summer rings

Each ring is one summer, May 1 at the top running clockwise to September 30; the oldest summer sits at the centre. The soft glow behind is each day’s heat. The bubbles are the weeks and days when people reacted more than usual: pink for ice cream searches, teal for AC searches, indigo for heat-related ER visits. The more intense the reaction, the bigger and deeper the bubble.

Size and depth of colour = where the day or week ranks among Detroit’s May–September values, 2022–2026 (the chosen threshold = smallest shown, the maximum = largest).
