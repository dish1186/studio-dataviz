Detroit summer rings

Each ring is one summer, May 1 at the top running clockwise to September 30; the oldest summer sits at the centre. The coloured background is each day's high, stepping darker at the temperatures where Detroit starts to act. The dots are events: weeks of unusually high ice cream or AC searching, and days of unusually high heat ER visits. Darker dots are stronger events; bigger dots mean that kind of event happened more often that month.

Thresholds for action (exploratory)
Events (dots)

Daily maximum temperature: gridMET (Climate Engine), Detroit, 2022-05-01 to 2026-09-24. Felt heat: daily maximum UTCI (to 2026-06-12). Events: above the 80th, 90th or 95th percentile of Detroit's May–September values, 2022–2026 (ER daily, HHS Region 5; searches weekly, Google Trends). Threshold temperatures are exploratory estimates from weekly Google Trends and CDC heat-related ER data (2022–2025): where ice cream searches, AC searches and heat ER visits (HHS Region 5) begin to rise. They are not yet a finalised analysis step.
