Heat signals over time

Weekly heat, felt heat, and three responses for one city, stacked on the same timeline. Shaded bands are summers (June to August). Hover anywhere to read the same week across every chart.

Weekly high: mean of 7 daily maxima (gridMET). Felt heat: mean of 7 daily UTCI maxima (ends 2026-06-12). Ice cream and AC: Google Trends weekly search interest for the metro, 0–100, each file scaled to its own peak, so only the shape compares across cities. Heat ER: heat-related illness ED visits per 100,000 ED visits, CDC Tracking Network, by HHS region (). Weeks of 2021-09-26 to 2026-09-20.
