Searches for “air purifier”, seven metros

Weekly Google Trends interest, Sep 2021 – Sep 2026. Each line is scaled to its own peak (100), so compare when cities spike, not how high.

Source: Google Trends, search term “air purifier”, All categories, past 5 years, weekly, downloaded by Gina 2026-10-01/02 (data/raw/google-trends/air-search-weekly/). Weeks with too little search volume are reported as 0 (most weeks in Eugene and Bakersfield). The shaded band marks a surge in all seven metros in spring 2026 with no local air-quality cause. 4-week average = centred mean of the week, the one before and the two after (Claude’s choice). The last, partial week (2026-09-27) is left out.
