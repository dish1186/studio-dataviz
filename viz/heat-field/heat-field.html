Heat and human reactions · May–September 2022–2026

Heat field

Every summer day sits in this field twice over: left to right by how unusual its high was for the date, bottom to top by how dangerous the heat felt to a human body. The soft blobs gather where people reacted strongly: heat-related ER visits, air-conditioner searches and ice cream searches.
